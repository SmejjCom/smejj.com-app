2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
smejj.com is a general AI assistant for everyday questions and for real work:
chat, voice input, code help, image and short-video generation, web search with
sources, and a built-in browser that can operate a website on request. Target
audience: adults who want one app instead of a drawer full of single-purpose
tools. The problem it solves: getting a clear answer or a small finished task
(text, code, picture, research) without learning a new tool.

3. HOW TO REACH THE MAIN FEATURES
No account is needed to review this app. Launch it, type any question into the
input field on the first screen and tap the arrow — the answer streams in right
away. Signing in is optional and only adds saved history, sync across devices
and file storage.
- Ask: first screen, input field, arrow button.
- Voice: microphone icon next to the input field.
- Images and short video: ask in plain words ("draw me a red bicycle").
- Web search with sources: ask about a current event; the sources appear under
  the answer.
- Built-in browser: globe icon in the header.
- Code area: menu, "Code".
- Report content: every AI answer and every generated image has a "..." menu
  with "Report content"; reports reach our moderation inbox.
- Optional sign-in: "Sign up free" — Google, GitHub, passkey, Sign in with Apple
  or a one-time e-mail link. There is no username/password login, which is why
  no demo credentials are listed — none are needed.
- Account deletion: EVERY sign-in method can delete the account inside the app,
  under Account > My data > "Delete account" (My data is the last of the nine
  tabs; on a phone, swipe the tab strip to its end). Type DELETE ACCOUNT to
  confirm; e-mail accounts also enter their password. All sessions end
  immediately and sign-in is disabled.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
- AI models: Z.ai (GLM), Moonshot AI (Kimi), Groq, and our own hosted model
  "smejj 1".
- Sign-in: Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, GitHub OAuth, WebAuthn passkeys,
  one-time e-mail links over our own SMTP.
- Storage: IDrive e2 (S3-compatible, EU region).
- Hosting: GitHub Pages (static files) and Zeabur (API).
- No payment processor inside the app, no advertising SDK, no third-party
  analytics or tracking SDK.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app behaves identically in every country. The interface follows the
device language (15 languages); features, content and limits do not differ by
region.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app is not in a regulated industry. It gives no medical, legal or financial
